## The Five Actions That Would Most Improve Your AI Footprint

These are listed in order of impact. You do not need to do all five -- each one independently raises your score.

### 1. Claim and Complete a Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile is the single highest-leverage action for local AI visibility. Google Gemini, which is integrated into Google Search, Android, and Google Maps, draws directly from GBP data. Meta AI and ChatGPT also reference Google Business data as a corroborating source. A complete GBP with your license number, service areas, business hours, photos, and review responses creates a structured entity that AI systems can parse immediately.

### 2. Build or Upgrade Your Personal Website with Schema Markup

A personal website is your owned real estate on the internet -- the one place where you control the narrative entirely. But a website without schema markup is like a billboard that only humans can read. AI systems need RealEstateAgent schema, LocalBusiness schema, and Review schema embedded in your site's code to understand who you are at a machine level. This is the single largest gap in your technical pillar, and the reason your technical score is currently negative.

### 3. Claim Profiles on Realtor.com, HomeLight, and Homes.com

Each of these platforms creates an independent, crawlable source of truth about you. AI systems use cross-platform consistency as a trust signal -- when the same agent appears on Zillow, Realtor.com, HomeLight, and Homes.com with consistent name, license number, and credentials, AI systems gain confidence that this is a real, active, verified professional. Three missing platforms means three missing corroborations.

### 4. Generate at Least One Press Mention or Community Feature

Press mentions are disproportionately weighted by AI systems because they represent third-party editorial validation. This does not need to be a national publication. A local business journal feature, a community board recognition, a charity event sponsorship mention, or even an interview in a neighborhood newsletter -- any independently published mention of your name in connection with real estate creates an authority signal that is very difficult for competitors to replicate.

### 5. Actively Solicit Recent Reviews on Multiple Platforms

AI systems treat review recency as a proxy for "is this agent currently active and performing well?" Reviews from 2023 count for very little in a March 2026 AI recommendation. Recent reviews on Zillow and Google (if you set up GBP) create fresh signals that AI systems pick up on their next crawl cycle. Volume matters, but recency matters more.
