## The Five Actions That Would Most Improve Your AI Footprint

These are listed in order of impact. You do not need to do all five -- each one independently raises your score.

### 1. Claim and Complete a Google Business Profile

This is the single highest-impact action available to you. Google Gemini serves more AI-assisted queries than any other system, and it pulls directly from GBP data for local professional recommendations. A complete GBP with your license number, service areas, business hours, photos, and review responses creates a structured entity that slots directly into Google's AI recommendation pipeline. Given your 30 years in market and 117 reviews, the absence of a GBP is your most costly gap.

### 2. Add Schema Markup to antonioalonzo.com

Your website exists and is crawlable -- it just doesn't speak the language AI systems understand. Schema markup (specifically RealEstateAgent, LocalBusiness, and AggregateRating types) embedded in your site's HTML tells AI systems exactly: who you are, where you work, what credentials you hold, how many reviews you have, and what your rating is. This transforms your website from a human-readable brochure into a machine-readable identity source. Your Technical pillar would jump from 1 to approximately 8-10 with proper schema implementation.

### 3. Standardize Your Name Across All Platforms

Decide on one professional name and make it consistent everywhere: Zillow, Realtor.com, LinkedIn, Yelp, your website, and any other profiles. AI systems use exact-match name consistency as a core entity resolution signal. Every variant ("Anthony" vs "Antonio" vs "Anthony Omar") creates friction in the AI's ability to aggregate your signals into a single entity. This is a zero-cost action that directly improves your Identity pillar.

### 4. Claim a HomeLight Profile

HomeLight is a high-authority agent evaluation platform that AI systems reference as independent validation. Claiming your profile and ensuring it reflects your current transaction data adds a corroborating signal that strengthens your Citability pillar. AI systems use the number of independent platforms confirming the same agent identity as a confidence multiplier.

### 5. Generate Fresh Reviews and at Least One Press Mention

Two separate actions, same underlying principle: AI systems weight recency above all else. A burst of 5-10 new reviews on Zillow and Google (once you have GBP) in the next 60 days would refresh your Social pillar significantly. Independently, a single press mention -- a local business feature, an award, a community sponsorship, an interview -- creates an authority signal that is very difficult for competitors to replicate. With 30 years in Northridge, the material for a press mention already exists; it just needs to be on the web in a form AI can crawl.
