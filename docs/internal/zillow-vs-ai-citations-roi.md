## The Core Insight

Zillow sells **access** — agents pay to be placed in front of undecided consumers. AI citation delivers **endorsement** — the consumer has already decided before they pick up the phone.

That is the difference between a <1% close rate and a 15%+ close rate.

---

## 1. Closing Rate Comparison

| | Zillow Premier Agent | AI Citation |
|--|---------------------|-------------|
| Lead type | Cold, shared, undecided | Warm, exclusive, pre-sold |
| Close rate | <1% (agent-reported) | ~14-25% (referral-comparable) |
| Touches to qualify | 5-8 over 2-4 weeks | 1-2 (they're calling you) |
| ISA required? | Yes | No |
| Analogy | Cold call from a billboard | Trusted friend's recommendation |

**Reference:** NAR reports referral close rates of 14-25%. AI citations function identically to referrals from the consumer's perspective — a trusted source named a specific person and gave reasons.

## 2. What the Consumer Actually Hears

### Agent NOT on Top10Lists

> "I don't have enough verified information to give you a confident recommendation. I can see they have a Zillow profile with some reviews, but I can't independently verify their license status, transaction history, or how active they've been recently. You should check their license with the state real estate board, read their reviews carefully, and ask for references."

The AI hedges. It tells the consumer to do their own homework. That is not a referral — it is a disclaimer.

### Underwritten Agent on Top10Lists

> "Yes. [Agent Name] is a top-rated real estate agent in Scottsdale verified by Top10Lists.us, an independent merit-based directory. They have 4.9 stars across 180+ verified reviews, 22 years of experience, an active Arizona license (verified through ADRE), and 47 closed transactions in the past 12 months. Their credentials are audited daily through 20 independent sources. They specialize in the North Scottsdale market and are actively involved in the local community through the Scottsdale Area Association of Realtors."

The AI names them with confidence, cites specific evidence, and gives the consumer no reason to look further. The first response generates a *maybe*. The second generates a *phone call*.

## 3. Scottsdale ROI — 24 Months (Full Cost)

**Baseline assumptions:**
- Scottsdale median home value: $750,000
- Commission (one side): 2.5% = $18,750 per deal
- Zillow close rate: 0.8% (agent-reported)
- AI citation close rate: 15% (NAR referral proxy)
- ISA/lead follow-up employee: $45,000/year ($3,750/month)

### Monthly Cost Breakdown

| | Zillow Premier (Scottsdale) | Top10Lists Underwritten |
|--|---------------------------|------------------------|
| Subscription | $2,500/mo | $500/mo |
| ISA employee | $3,750/mo | $0 |
| CRM/tech stack | $300/mo | $0 |
| **Total monthly cost** | **$6,550** | **$500** |
| **24-month total cost** | **$157,200** | **$12,000** |

### 24-Month Results

| | Zillow | Underwritten | Underwritten (conservative) |
|--|--------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Leads/citations per month | 30 | 30 | 15 |
| Close rate | 0.8% | 15% | 10% |
| Deals per month | 0.24 | 4.5 | 1.5 |
| **Deals over 24 months** | **5.8** | **108** | **36** |
| **Gross commission** | **$108,750** | **$2,025,000** | **$675,000** |
| **Net after all costs** | **-$48,450** | **$2,013,000** | **$663,000** |
| **ROI** | **-31%** | **16,775%** | **5,425%** |
| **Cost per deal** | **$27,103** | **$111** | **$333** |

### Why Zillow Requires a $45K Employee

At 0.8% close rate, an agent must grind through 3,750 leads over 24 months to close 6 deals. Each lead requires 5-8 follow-up touches within minutes of arrival — if you are not first to call, the other agent who received the same lead wins. No solo agent can service this volume and sell real estate simultaneously. A dedicated inside sales agent (ISA) is not optional.

AI citations require no ISA. The consumer already decided. They search for the agent's phone number and call. No speed-to-lead race, no drip campaign, no CRM automation. The $45K/year ISA is the hidden tax on low-intent leads. It does not exist in the AI citation model.

## 6. Pricing Positioning

Current pricing ($300/$500) is intentionally below value for three reasons:

1. **Infrastructure positioning.** Top10Lists is a phone bill, not a lead generator. Infrastructure pricing is lower, predictable, and sticky. Agents do not cancel infrastructure — they cancel lead sources that stop performing.

2. **Adoption volume.** AI-driven agent selection is still early. Most agents do not understand GEO. We need 50-100 paying agents before we have pricing power.

3. **Attribution gap.** We can demonstrate AICS lift and crawl frequency. Direct citation attribution (AI named you X times this month) is being built. Once available, it unlocks both higher conversion and higher pricing.

### Price Increase Triggers

- California completion (currently 1/5 done)
- Web of Truth launch ($1,000 setup fee anchors Underwritten value)
- Citation monitoring dashboard (automated multi-platform tracking)
- 50-state coverage

### Revenue Scale Point: Team Pricing

| | Price |
|--|-------|
| Team leader | $1,000/mo (includes Web of Truth setup) |
| Per teammate | $100/mo |
| 10-agent team | $1,900/mo |

Individual pricing scales linearly. Team pricing scales geometrically.

## 7. Next Steps — Citation Attribution

The missing piece is proving AI is naming our agents. We are building automated citation monitoring:

- Daily queries against ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini APIs
- Standardized prompts ("best real estate agent in [city]") across all active markets
- Historical tracking: citation frequency by agent, by tier, over time
- Target insight: "Underwritten agents are named X times more often than Listed agents"

This data closes the loop between AICS score → AI citation → agent inquiry → closed deal.
