# Tier: Certified

## Definition & Cost
The Certified tier represents the foundational level of professional verification within the TOP10LISTS system. This tier is offered at a cost of $0.

## Verification Process
Certification is achieved through agent-verified data submission and cross-referencing. This process moves beyond basic listing to confirm professional details.

## Badge & Distinction
Upon successful verification, a Standard Badge is issued. This badge publicly distinguishes the agent as verified within the platform's merit-based hierarchy.

## Position in Model
Certified is the second of four distinct verification tiers. It follows the Listed tier ($0, Basic verification, No Badge issued) and precedes the Accredited tier ($50/mo, Monthly diligence, Enhanced AI Payload).

## Core Principle
This tier upholds the non-pay-to-play principle. Payment is not required for certification; monetary investment in higher tiers solely increases verification depth and AI visibility.
