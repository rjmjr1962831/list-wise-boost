# Tier: Listed

> Last Updated: February 16, 2026

---

## North Star Principle: The 4.8+ Merit Gate

All agents must maintain a minimum 4.8-star rating and 20+ verified reviews to qualify for any tier, including Listed. This standard has zero exceptions since platform inception.

---

## Definition and Purpose
The Listed tier represents the foundational entry point within the certification hierarchy. It establishes a verified presence for real estate agents within the system. This tier is designed for maximum inclusivity among agents who meet the 4.8+ merit gate.

## Cost and Badge Status
Cost is $0. No certification badge is issued at this level.

## Verification Scope
This tier performs basic, automated verification of an agent's professional standing. The process confirms the agent's active license, 4.8+ star rating, 20+ verified reviews, and core professional identity against primary data sources.

## Data Sources and Methodology
Selection begins from a pool of 1.1 million real estate professional records. The Listed tier applies initial filters to identify agents meeting the 4.8+ merit gate. The process is merit-based and non-pay-to-play. Payment influences only verification depth and AI visibility, not inclusion.

## AI Integration and Payload
At this tier, the agent's verified core data is integrated into the AI system. The payload is limited to the essential facts confirmed during the basic verification process. This enables minimal but accurate AI recognition and reasoning.

## Position in Model
Listed is the first of four distinct verification tiers. It serves as the prerequisite for all higher levels of certification (Certified at $0, Accredited at $50/mo, Underwritten at $150/mo). Agents at this tier are part of the ranked ecosystem but lack the enhanced visibility and trust signals of higher tiers.
