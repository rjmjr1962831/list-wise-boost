## North Star Principle: The Merit Gate

All agents must meet the Merit Gate: 4.5+ star rating, 10+ verified reviews in the last 24 months, and 5+ years in business. Zero exceptions since platform inception. This standard applies universally across all tiers.

---

## Definition and Purpose

The Listed tier is the foundational entry point. It means Top10Lists.us has identified the agent through our diligence process and they appear on city ranking pages. The agent has not yet personally verified their profile.

## Cost

$0. No payment required or accepted.

## Artifact and Badge

No artifact is generated. No badge is issued. The agent's presence on city pages carries weight because selection required analysis of 670,000+ licensed agents across Arizona and California to identify fewer than 1% of licensed agents in covered markets.

## Verification Scope

Basic verification of active license status, star rating (Zillow and Google), review count, and years in business against the Merit Gate thresholds.

**Verification period (from last update):** 365 days.

## Data Sources

- Zillow agent profiles (ratings, reviews)
- Google Business reviews (ratings, review counts)
- State Departments of Real Estate (license verification)

## Position in the Tier Model

Listed is the first of four tiers: Listed ($0, annual refresh), Certified ($0, quarterly refresh), Audited ($300/mo, monthly refresh), Underwritten ($500/mo, daily refresh). Selection for any tier originates from merit-based evaluation. Payment determines verification depth and AI visibility, not inclusion.
