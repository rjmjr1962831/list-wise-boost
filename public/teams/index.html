How it works

Every agent on your roster is evaluated against the same merit gate that applies to individual applicants: 4.5+ stars, 10+ verified reviews in 24 months, 5+ years experience, active license. The difference is that qualifying agents are onboarded under a single agreement with team-level data architecture that reinforces each individual citation.

The same merit gate applies. Payment does not waive qualification criteria for any agent on the roster. What it does is provide deeper verification, faster refresh cycles, and structured data architecture that makes the entire team more citable as a unit.

What team leaders get

A single agreement covering all qualifying agents. Enterprise-level data architecture that links team members under a brokerage identity. Citation monitoring across AI platforms. A dedicated implementation contact for onboarding and ongoing performance review.

Team pricing starts at $1,000/month for the leader (includes Web of Truth setup), plus $100/month per teammate badge. Annual commitment waives the Web of Truth setup fee.
