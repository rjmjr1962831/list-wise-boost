Claremont Real Estate Market Intelligence

Claremont rises at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains in eastern Los Angeles County, a college town of 37,000 known for the Claremont Colleges consortium and its tree-lined village downtown. This city of trees and PhD's maintains small-town character while hosting five undergraduate and two graduate institutions.

History

Founded in 1887 as a colony of the Pacific Land Improvement Company.

Pomona College established in 1887, with other Claremont Colleges following.

Became California's first Arbor Day Foundation Tree City USA in 1976.

Life in Claremont

Academic atmosphere permeates the community, with professors, students, and graduates shaping civic life. The Village maintains independent shops against chain encroachment. Tree preservation defines community identity.

Buyer Profile

College faculty and staff. Families prioritizing schools. Retirees seeking walkability and culture. Intellectuals attracted to college town atmosphere.

Market Trends

Median around $850,000-950,000. Village-adjacent properties at premium. 35-45 days on market. Strong demand from college community.

Local Insider Tip

The Folk Music Center, where the Chases have sold instruments and hosted performances since 1958, remains a pilgrimage site for musicians and a genuine Claremont institution.

Why People Move to Claremont

Academic Community: Five colleges create intellectual culture.

Village Character: Walkable downtown with independent shops.

Tree City: Extensive urban forest.

Mountain Access: Gateway to Mount Baldy and San Gabriels.
