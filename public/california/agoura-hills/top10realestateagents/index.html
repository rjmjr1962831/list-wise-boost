Agoura Hills Real Estate Market Intelligence

Agoura Hills nestles in the Santa Monica Mountains at the western edge of Los Angeles County, offering a rare combination of suburban tranquility and natural beauty within commuting distance of LA and the San Fernando Valley. The city emerged from rural ranch land in the 1980s with master-planned developments preserving open space and mountain views. History

The area was originally part of Rancho Las Virgenes, a Mexican land grant from 1834 covering over 8,000 acres.

Agoura Hills incorporated in 1982 to control development and preserve the semi-rural character threatened by Los Angeles sprawl.

The 2018 Woolsey Fire burned through portions of the community, reshaping building codes and fire safety awareness.

Life in Agoura Hills

Agoura Hills attracts families and professionals seeking suburban quality with outdoor access. The equestrian heritage remains strong with horse properties and riding trails throughout. Residents fiercely protect the semi-rural character against density, while appreciating the excellent schools and safe neighborhoods. The community rebuilt together after the Woolsey Fire, strengthening local bonds.
