The city transformed from agricultural and industrial roots into a bedroom community as Bay Area prices pushed buyers eastward. History

Antioch was founded in 1850 by twin brothers from New Hampshire who named it after the biblical city.

The city developed around coal mining and later became a major producer of gypsum and chemicals.

BART extension to Antioch opened in 2018, dramatically improving commute options to San Francisco and Oakland.

Life in Antioch

Antioch attracts families seeking affordable homeownership within Bay Area commuting range, accepting longer commutes for more space and lower prices. The Delta lifestyle appeals to boating enthusiasts and anglers. Long-time residents remember the industrial past while newcomers reshape neighborhoods. The community balances growth pressure against infrastructure capacity.

Buyer Profile

First-time buyers priced out of inner Bay Area communities dominate demand. Families accept commute times for yard space and homeownership. Some buyers seek Delta waterfront living. Investors find rental yields attractive.

Market Trends

Antioch median prices hover around $575,000, representing exceptional Bay Area value. Days on market average 28 days. Waterfront and newer construction command premiums.

Local Insider Tip
