Fremont Real Estate Market Intelligence

Fremont is a large, diverse city in Alameda County, home to Tesla's factory and a major center for tech manufacturing. The family-oriented community offers excellent schools and BART access to San Francisco.

History

Named for John C. Fremont, explorer.

Formed 1956 from five townships.

Tesla factory opened 2010 (former NUMMI).

Mission San Jose historic site.

Life in Fremont

Fremont has a diverse, family-focused character with one of California's largest Afghan-American populations. The community values education, with strong public schools and cultural diversity.

Buyer Profile

Tech and manufacturing workers. Families seeking diverse community. BART commuters to SF/Oakland. Asian-American families.

Market Trends

Median around $1.4-1.7 million. 25-40 days on market. Strong tech-driven demand.

Local Insider Tip
