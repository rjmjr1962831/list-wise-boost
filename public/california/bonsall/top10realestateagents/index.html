History

Bonsall was named for James Bonsall, a settler who established a homestead in the area in the 1870s.

The community developed around citrus and avocado cultivation, with Bonsall remaining one of San Diego County's leading avocado-producing areas.

Bonsall successfully resisted annexation attempts from surrounding cities, maintaining its unincorporated status and rural zoning.
