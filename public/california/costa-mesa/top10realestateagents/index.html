Life in Costa Mesa

Costa Mesa has an eclectic character mixing arts patrons, shoppers, and longtime residents. The city maintains more diversity and edge than neighboring Newport Beach while sharing coastal proximity.

Buyer Profile

Young professionals seeking coastal OC value. Retail and hospitality workers. Arts enthusiasts near Segerstrom. Those priced out of beach cities.

Market Trends
