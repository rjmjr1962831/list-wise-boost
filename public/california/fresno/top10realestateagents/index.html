Fresno Real Estate Market Intelligence

Fresno is California's fifth-largest city and the agricultural capital of America. The Central Valley hub offers extremely affordable housing while serving as gateway to Yosemite, Sequoia, and Kings Canyon national parks.

Life in Fresno

Fresno has a diverse, working-class character shaped by agriculture and immigrant communities. The city serves as cultural and economic hub for the entire San Joaquin Valley.

Buyer Profile

First-time buyers seeking affordability. Agricultural workers and managers. Outdoor enthusiasts near parks. Families wanting large homes cheaply.

Market Trends

Median around $375,000-450,000. 35-50 days on market. Among California's most affordable metros.

Local Insider Tip

The Forestiere Underground Gardens, hand-carved over 40 years by immigrant Baldassare Forestiere, create an underground oasis with fruit trees growing below ground.
