Dana Point Real Estate Market Intelligence

Dana Point is a coastal city in Orange County built around a picturesque harbor and dramatic bluffs. The upscale community offers beach lifestyle with world-class whale watching and the historic charm of a former surfing mecca.

History

Named for Richard Henry Dana, author.

Developed as planned community 1970s.

Harbor completed 1971.

Doheny State Beach historic surf spot.

Life in Dana Point

Dana Point maintains a relaxed coastal character where harbor life and ocean activities define daily rhythm. The community balances tourism with residential tranquility, attracting those seeking authentic beach town feel.

Buyer Profile

Affluent buyers seeking harbor lifestyle. Boating enthusiasts. Families wanting beach community. Retirees preferring quieter coast.

Market Trends

Median around $1.5-2.0 million. 45-65 days on market. Premium for harbor proximity.

Local Insider Tip

Dana Point is California's official Whale Watching Capital, with gray whale migrations passing so close that harbor tours offer near-guaranteed sightings.
