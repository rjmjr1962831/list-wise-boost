History

Graeagle was originally a company town founded in 1917 by the Feather River Lumber Company, which operated a large sawmill on the Middle Fork of the Feather River.

The Graeagle post office opened in 1920, and the town's name is a contraction of 'Gray Eagle,' the mining company that originally owned the land.

The historic Graeagle Mill Pond, now a scenic centerpiece, was created to hold logs for the lumber mill and is a remnant of this industrial past.
