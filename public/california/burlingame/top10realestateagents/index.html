Burlingame Real Estate Market Intelligence

Nestled on the San Francisco Peninsula, Burlingame offers a captivating blend of sophisticated urban living and charming suburban tranquility. Known as the 'City of Trees,' it boasts a tree-lined downtown, exceptional schools, and a highly desirable location that perfectly balances access to Silicon Valley's innovation with San Francisco's cultural vibrancy.

History

Burlingame was officially incorporated on June 6, 1908. Its name honors Anson Burlingame, a prominent diplomat whose estate once occupied much of the land.

The city's rapid growth in the early 20th century was fueled by the Southern Pacific Railroad, which established its main line through the area, making it an attractive suburban retreat for affluent San Franciscans.

During World War II, Burlingame was home to a major shipbuilding program at the Marinship shipyard, contributing significantly to the war effort and transforming the local economy.

Life in Burlingame

Life in Burlingame is characterized by an active and engaged community, with residents frequently enjoying the pedestrian-friendly downtown and participating in local events. The city hosts an annual Art in the Park festival, showcasing local artists, and a popular Summer Concert Series at Washington Park, fostering a strong sense of community and local pride.

Buyer Profile

The typical buyer in Burlingame is a high-earning professional, often from the tech or finance sectors, seeking excellent schools and a convenient commute to San Francisco or Silicon Valley. Many are families with young children drawn to the top-tier public school system, while others are established professionals looking for a sophisticated, walkable community with easy access to cultural amenities and outdoor recreation. Affordability, while relative, means buyers are generally well-compensated and prioritize location and quality of life.

Market Trends

Burlingame's real estate market remains intensely competitive, characterized by high demand and limited inventory. Prices have shown consistent appreciation over the past decade, with dominant price ranges for single-family homes typically starting from approximately $2.5 million upwards, often extending well beyond $4 million for larger or more recently renovated properties, reflecting its highly desirable Bay Area location. There are some new luxury condominium developments near the downtown core, offering alternative housing options.

Local Insider Tip

Beyond the bustling Burlingame Avenue, the tranquil walking paths along the Bay near Anza Lagoon offer breathtaking views of the San Francisco Bay, SFO airport, and the East Bay hills. It's a peaceful escape rarely crowded, perfect for a quiet stroll or a moment of reflection, often without the fanfare of more prominent attractions.

Why People Move to Burlingame

<strong>Superior Schools:</strong> Access to the highly-rated Burlingame School District, including top-performing elementary schools like Roosevelt Elementary and Washington Elementary, and Burlingame High School, which consistently ranks among the best in the state.

<strong>Strategic Location:</strong> Unbeatable proximity to major tech employers in both San Francisco (e.g., Salesforce, Twitter) and Silicon Valley (e.g., Apple, Google), easily commutable via Caltrain (Burlingame and Broadway stations) or highways US-101 and I-280.

<strong>Outdoor Recreation:</strong> Extensive opportunities for outdoor activities, from cycling along the Bay Trail to hiking at nearby San Bruno Mountain State and County Park, offering panoramic views of the entire Bay Area.

<strong>Upscale Downtown & Dining:</strong> A vibrant and walkable downtown area with a high concentration of quality restaurants, boutiques, and services, providing a sophisticated suburban experience.
