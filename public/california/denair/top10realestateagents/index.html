History

Denair was founded in 1906 as a railroad town along the Southern Pacific line, originally named 'Elmwood' before being renamed for its first postmaster, John Denair.

The Denair Depot, built in 1910, is a historic landmark that served as the town's commercial and social hub for decades and remains a focal point of community identity.

Historically an agricultural community, Denair's economy was built around dairy farming, almond orchards, and grain production, a heritage still visible in the surrounding landscape.
