History

Belvedere was developed in the 1890s as a summer resort for wealthy San Franciscans who arrived by ferry to escape the city fog.

The Belvedere Land Company subdivided the island in 1890, establishing the pattern of hillside lots with views that defines the community today.

Belvedere incorporated as a city in 1896, making it one of the oldest municipalities in Marin County.

Life in Belvedere

Belvedere embodies Marin County's blend of natural beauty and affluent sophistication. Residents value privacy while supporting local institutions through philanthropy and volunteerism. Social life centers on yacht clubs, school activities, and the tennis courts.
