History

Hydesville was founded in the late 19th century and was named after John Hyde, an early settler and landowner in the area.

The community's early economy was heavily based on timber and agriculture, particularly dairy farming, which shaped its rural character.

Hydesville gained broader notoriety in 1998 as the location of the tragic school bus kidnapping, an event that led to significant changes in national child safety laws and Amber Alert systems.
