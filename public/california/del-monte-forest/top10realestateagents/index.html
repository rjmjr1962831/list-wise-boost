Del Monte Forest Real Estate Market Intelligence

Del Monte Forest is an exclusive, privately-owned residential community located on the Monterey Peninsula in California. It is renowned for its breathtaking coastal scenery, world-class golf courses, and meticulously preserved natural environment, including the iconic 17-Mile Drive. The real estate market here is one of the most prestigious and tightly held in the nation, characterized by ultra-luxury properties, sprawling estates on large wooded lots, and architectural gems designed to maximize ocean and forest views. Inventory is perpetually low, and transactions occur within a rarefied sphere, often off-market, attracting discerning buyers seeking privacy, unparalleled beauty, and a legacy investment.

History

The forest and surrounding land were part of the vast Mexican land grant Rancho Punta de Pinos, purchased by the Pacific Improvement Company in the late 19th century.

The world-famous 17-Mile Drive was originally created in 1881 as a scenic carriage road for guests of the Hotel Del Monte, connecting the hotel to the Pacific Grove coastline.

The Del Monte Forest is home to the iconic Lone Cypress, a Monterey cypress tree that has been perched on a granite headland for over 250 years and is a registered trademark of the Pebble Beach Company.
