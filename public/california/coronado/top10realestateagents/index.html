Coronado Real Estate Market Intelligence

Coronado is an affluent resort city on a peninsula in San Diego Bay, connected to San Diego by the iconic Coronado Bridge. The prestigious community offers beach living, historic architecture, and military presence at North Island Naval Air Station.

Life in Coronado

Coronado has an upscale, resort-town character where military families mix with affluent civilians. The community maintains small-town feel despite tourism, with strong civic pride and preserved historic character.

Buyer Profile

Affluent buyers seeking beach lifestyle. Military families at North Island. Retirees wanting prestigious address. Second-home buyers from inland.

Market Trends

Median around $2.0-2.8 million. 45-70 days on market. Premium for limited peninsula inventory.
