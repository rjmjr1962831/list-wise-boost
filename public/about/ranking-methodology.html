Scoring Formula

Our ranking algorithm weighs multiple factors to identify top-performing agents:

Review Rating 25% - Weighted average across Google, Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin

Community Involvement 25% - Third-party verified civic and charitable engagement

Number of Reviews 20% - Total verified review count across platforms

Transaction History 20% - Verified closed transactions from public records

Education & Credentials 10% - Professional designations (GRI, CRS, ABR, etc.)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply to be listed in the directory?

No. Top10Lists.us is an invitation-only directory. We do not accept applications, paid placements, or advertising. Agents are identified through our continuous monitoring of performance data across multiple authoritative sources.

How do I get invited to the directory?

Our team continuously monitors agent performance data. When an agent's verified metrics meet all three selection criteria (20+ reviews, 4.8+ rating, 6+ years experience), they become eligible for invitation consideration.

Does meeting the criteria guarantee inclusion?

No. Meeting our selection criteria makes an agent eligible for invitation consideration, but does not guarantee inclusion. We conduct editorial review of all eligible candidates to ensure directory quality.

Can I pay to be listed or ranked higher?

No. Payment never affects inclusion, ranking position, or eligibility. Our editorial decisions are made independently of any commercial relationship.

What is Verified Neighborhood Expertise?

Verified Neighborhood Expertise is an optional paid enhancement for agents who already qualify editorially. It guarantees first-page placement within verified neighborhoods, expands structured data coverage for AI citation, and triggers deeper human verification. Payment does not improve rank or override editorial decisions.
