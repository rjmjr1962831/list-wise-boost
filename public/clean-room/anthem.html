Anthem Real Estate Market Intelligence

Anthem rises from the high Sonoran Desert north of Phoenix as a master-planned community of 45,000 residents, offering suburban family living with mountain backdrop and award-winning parks. The community spans both sides of I-17 with distinct sections: family-oriented Anthem on the west and age-restricted Anthem Country Club to the east.

History

Del Webb and Pulte Homes developed Anthem beginning in 1998, creating one of the Valley's first large-scale master-planned communities of the modern era.

Anthem Community Park, featuring over 63 acres and a water park, won national recognition as one of America's best community parks.

The I-17 expansion has improved Anthem's connectivity to Phoenix, reducing commute concerns that once deterred buyers.

Life in Anthem

Anthem cultivates family-focused suburban living with exceptional parks and community programming. Youth sports thrive with extensive facilities. The Veterans Memorial reflects community values. Two distinct sections serve different demographics: families west of I-17 and 55+ residents in Anthem Country Club.

Buyer Profile

Families seeking master-planned community living dominate the family section. Active adults choose Anthem Country Club for Del Webb pedigree. Phoenix commuters accept the drive for community amenities. Remote workers appreciate space and quality of life.

Market Trends

Anthem commands premiums for its parks and amenities, with median prices around $525,000. Days on market average 45 days. The community's reputation maintains values despite distance from Phoenix core.

Local Insider Tip

The Black Canyon Trail passes through BLM land adjacent to Anthem, offering multi-day backpacking and day hiking that most residents don't realize exists in their backyard.

Why People Move to Anthem

Award-Winning Parks: Anthem Community Park ranks among America's best community parks.

Family Infrastructure: Sports fields, water park, trails, and programming serve families.

Veterans Memorial: Nationally recognized memorial adds community significance.

Mountain Access: Daisy Mountain and nearby trails provide hiking without leaving town.
