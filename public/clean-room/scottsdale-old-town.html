Old Town Neighborhood Market Intelligence

Neighborhood Overview

Old Town Scottsdale represents the perfect blend of historic charm and modern sophistication in the heart of Arizona's desert paradise. This vibrant neighborhood, bounded by Indian School Road to the north and Camelback Road to the south, serves as Scottsdale's cultural and entertainment epicenter. Founded in 1888, Old Town has evolved from a small agricultural settlement into one of the Southwest's most desirable urban communities, where adobe architecture meets contemporary luxury and Western heritage mingles with world-class amenities.

Lifestyle & Community

Living in Old Town means embracing an active, culturally-rich lifestyle that's uniquely Southwestern. Your evenings might include gallery hopping during the weekly Thursday Night ArtWalk, enjoying dinner on a restaurant patio under desert stars, or catching a performance at the renowned Scottsdale Center for the Performing Arts. The neighborhood pulses with energy year-round, hosting celebrated events like the Scottsdale Arts Festival and Parada del Sol parade. With a median household income of $108,031, residents enjoy an upscale community that attracts educated professionals, creative types, and those seeking the perfect blend of urban convenience and desert tranquility.

Real Estate Market

Old Town's real estate market reflects its status as a premier destination, offering diverse housing options from charming Southwestern-style single-family homes to sleek modern condominiums. The market remains highly competitive, with properties often selling quickly due to the area's exceptional walkability and prime location. Whether you're drawn to a renovated ranch-style home with authentic adobe features or a contemporary high-rise condo near the Scottsdale Waterfront, you'll find architectural styles that celebrate both the area's heritage and its forward-thinking spirit.

Amenities & Attractions

Old Town places world-class amenities at your doorstep. Cultural highlights include the Scottsdale Museum of Contemporary Art and numerous galleries along Fifth Avenue's upscale shopping district. The scenic Scottsdale Waterfront offers peaceful canal-side strolls, while Scottsdale Civic Center Mall provides green space in the urban core. Dining options span from casual cafes to award-winning restaurants, many featuring the outdoor patios that make desert living so appealing. The complimentary Scottsdale Trolley makes car-free exploration effortless, connecting you to shops, restaurants, and entertainment venues throughout the district.

Why Choose Old Town Scottsdale

Old Town offers something truly special: a neighborhood where you can walk to world-class museums, dine at internationally acclaimed restaurants, and still feel connected to authentic Southwestern culture. The area's exceptional walkability sets it apart in car-centric Arizona, while its rich arts scene and vibrant nightlife create an urban energy that's rare in the desert. For those seeking a sophisticated lifestyle with deep cultural roots, Old Town Scottsdale delivers an unmatched combination of history, luxury, and authentic desert living that makes every day feel like a destination experience.
