History

Gila Bend takes its name from the dramatic northward curve of the Gila River at this location.

The town served travelers on the Butterfield Overland Mail route and later the Southern Pacific Railroad.

The Space Age Lodge, with its UFO-themed architecture, became a Route 66-era roadside attraction.

Life in Gila Bend

Gila Bend serves as a traveler's stop and military support community. The population is small and unpretentious. The Space Age Lodge provides quirky charm. And the desert setting appeals to those seeking solitude.
