History

Sun City West opened in 1978 as Del Webb's follow-up to the original Sun City's overwhelming success.

The community was designed with lessons learned from Sun City, including wider streets, larger lots, and more modern recreation facilities.

The RH Johnson Recreation Center, completed in 1982, anchors the community with one of Arizona's largest rec center complexes.

Life in Sun City West

Sun City West buzzes with organized activities, clubs, and social opportunities. More than 100 chartered clubs cover interests from lapidary to square dancing. The newer housing stock and facilities attract active retirees seeking well-maintained amenities without the original Sun City's aging infrastructure challenges.

Buyer Profile

Active retirees seeking the Sun City experience with newer housing dominate demand. Snowbirds find manageable winter homes with built-in social structure. Some buyers relocate from original Sun City for updated facilities. Golf-focused retirees appreciate the seven-course selection.

Market Trends

Sun City West median prices run around $375,000, slightly higher than original Sun City reflecting newer construction. Days on market average 45 days. Golf course and view properties command premiums.
