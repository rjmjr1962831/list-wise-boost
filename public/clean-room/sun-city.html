Sun City Real Estate Market Intelligence

Sun City stands as the original Del Webb active adult community, the prototype that launched a nationwide retirement revolution when it opened on New Year's Day 1960. Now home to 40,000 residents aged 55+, Sun City offers an extraordinary concentration of recreation centers, golf courses, and community activities in a meticulously maintained environment.

History

Del Webb opened Sun City on January 1, 1960, expecting 10,000 visitors. Over 100,000 people showed up, validating Webb's vision of active retirement living.

Sun City pioneered the age-restricted community concept, establishing the 55+ requirement that has defined active adult living nationwide.

The community was designed around recreation and activity with the slogan 'An Active Way of Life.'

Life in Sun City

Sun City cultivates active retirement with devotion. Residents keep busy with golf, tennis, swimming, crafts, and organized activities. The atmosphere is relentlessly positive with well-maintained homes and community standards.

Buyer Profile

Active retirees seeking community and recreation anchor the market. Snowbirds seeking winter homes find activities fill seasonal stays. Budget-conscious retirees find prices below newer active adult communities.

Market Trends

Sun City offers affordable active adult living with median prices around $320,000. Days on market average 55 days. Many homes need updating, creating opportunities for renovation.

Local Insider Tip

The Sun City library system operates seven branches with extensive collections rivaling many public systems.

Why People Move to Sun City

Recreation Paradise: Seven rec centers, eight golf courses, and over 100 clubs.

Established Community: Over 60 years of development means mature infrastructure.

Affordability: Older housing stock creates prices below surrounding communities.

Location: Edge of metro Phoenix with easy access to shopping and healthcare.
