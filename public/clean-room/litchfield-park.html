History

Litchfield Park was founded in 1916 by Paul Litchfield of Goodyear Tire as a company town for cotton production to supply tire cord fabric.

The Wigwam resort opened in 1929 as an executive retreat and has hosted presidents, celebrities, and dignitaries for nearly a century.

Litchfield Park incorporated in 1987 specifically to preserve its historic character as Goodyear development surrounded it.

Life in Litchfield Park

Litchfield Park maintains small-town character within the booming West Valley, with residents fiercely protective of the historic atmosphere. The Wigwam resort brings visitors and hospitality employment. Community events center on the downtown village, and the tree canopy distinguishes the city from surrounding desert developments.
