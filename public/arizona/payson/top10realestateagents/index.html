History

Payson began as a gold mining camp in 1881, named after Illinois Senator Louis Payson who helped establish a post office for the remote settlement.

Zane Grey, the famous Western novelist, built a cabin near Payson in 1920, drawing inspiration from the Tonto Basin for many of his stories.

The Beeline Highway (SR 87) connected Payson to Phoenix in 1959, transforming the isolated community into a Phoenix escape destination.
