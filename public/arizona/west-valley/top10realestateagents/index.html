History

The West Valley remained cotton fields and cattle ranches until the 1990s when development pressure from Phoenix pushed westward.

Luke Air Force Base, established in 1941, anchored the economy before residential development arrived.

The Loop 303 freeway opening in the 2000s transformed the region's accessibility and accelerated growth dramatically.
