History

Winslow was established as a railroad town in 1881, becoming a major division point on the Santa Fe Railway.

The La Posada Hotel, designed by Mary Colter and opened in 1930, was the last great Fred Harvey railroad hotel.

The Eagles' 1972 song 'Take It Easy' put Winslow on the map with the lyric 'Standing on a corner in Winslow, Arizona.'

Life in Winslow

Winslow embraces its Route 66 and Eagles song fame while maintaining small-town character. La Posada's restoration has brought visitors and cultural events. The railroad continues to run through town. And the community balances tourism with practical small-town life.
