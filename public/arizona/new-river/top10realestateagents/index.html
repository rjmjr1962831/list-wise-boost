History

New River takes its name from the intermittent waterway that flows north to the Verde River, unusual in a region where most drainages flow south.

The area remained ranching territory until the 1970s when rural residential development began attracting those fleeing Phoenix density.

Lack of incorporation has been repeatedly rejected by residents preferring county governance over municipal regulations.

Life in New River

New River attracts people who want acreage, animals, and autonomy. Horse property is common, with many residents keeping multiple animals. The lack of streetlights keeps dark skies for stargazing. The community gathers at local establishments along New River Road, maintaining small-town feel despite Phoenix proximity.
