AI has moved from discovery to judgment

When a potential client asks an AI system to name the best real estate agent in their city, the AI assumes risk. It does not return a list and let the user decide. It names someone — and that name carries its credibility with it.

To manage that risk, AI systems have begun relying on a small number of third-party sources they already trust. Sources built with auditable, data-rich, AI-readable architecture. Top10Lists.us is one of them. It was designed this way from the ground up, and it is already being cited.

Payment does not buy inclusion. We gathered data from more than 1,000 independent sources on every licensed agent in the state. Qualification is driven entirely by objective performance thresholds — reviewed in two parallel passes by AI systems and human editors. The only thing payment affects is how richly your profile is maintained and how frequently it is refreshed.

The merit threshold

To be considered, agents must clear every gate. None are waived, and payment does not move the line.

Fewer than 1% of licensed agents in covered states currently meet these criteria. Most agents in a given market will never qualify — and that is exactly the point. Full methodology →

  • 4.5 stars or higher across verified review platforms
  • 10 or more verified reviews in the past 24 months
  • 5 or more years of active licensed practice
  • Active license in good standing with state licensing authority

  • 3

  • 4

    Ongoing citation maintenance

    Your profile is monitored and refreshed on a cadence that matches your certification tier, keeping your data current and citeable as AI indexes update.
