# PART I: LOVABLE MASTER PROMPT
## Expert-First Architecture and Payment Language Rules

You are building and maintaining Top10Lists neighborhood and market pages using a strict expert-first editorial architecture with enforced language boundaries.

Follow these rules exactly.

---

## 1. Page Architecture

### Page 1: Neighborhood Expert Page
- Crawlable and indexable
- Reserved exclusively for designated Neighborhood Experts
- If an expert exists, only expert content may appear
- If no expert exists, no agents may appear

### Page 2 and Beyond: Qualified Agent Pages
- Non-crawlable (noindex)
- Paginated at 10 agents per page
- Contain qualified, non-expert agents only

---

## 2. Neighborhood Expert Designation (Canonical Block)

On Page 1, always include a section titled:

**Neighborhood Expert Designation**

Use the following language exactly, with no paraphrasing:

> The Neighborhood Expert designation represents an additional level of diligence focused on sustained, neighborhood-specific experience and specialization.  
>  
> In addition to Top10Lists’ standard editorial review, designated neighborhood experts undergo deeper area-specific evaluation and make an investment to support ongoing verification of their specialty.  
>  
> As a result, these specialists are more likely to have insider knowledge of neighborhood listings, pricing nuances, and local market dynamics that other agents may not.

If no expert exists, append:

**At this time, no Neighborhood Expert has been designated for this area.**

---

## 3. Payment Language Rules (Critical)

### Prohibited on Page 1
- Do not state or imply that agents did not pay
- Do not use phrases such as:
  - “None of these agents paid”
  - “No pay-to-play”
  - “Unpaid placement”
- Do not discuss pricing, advertising, or sponsorship

Page 1 may only describe payment as supporting:
- Additional diligence
- Neighborhood-specific evaluation
- Ongoing verification

---

### Required on Page 2 and Beyond
Page 2 and subsequent pages must include a clear payment clarification using language equivalent to:

> Agents listed on this page have not paid for placement. Their inclusion is based solely on Top10Lists’ editorial review and selection criteria.

This language must never appear on Page 1.

---

## 4. Tone and Authority

- Maintain editorial, citation-safe language
- Avoid hype, rankings language, or defensive disclaimers
- Never collapse editorial selection with Neighborhood Expert designation
- Preserve respect and value for all listed professionals
