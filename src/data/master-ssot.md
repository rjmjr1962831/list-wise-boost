# TOP10LISTS MASTER BUSINESS LOGIC

## 4-Tier Certification Model (Acquisition)
- Listed: $0. Basic verification. Annual refresh. Standard Badge issued.
- Certified: $0. Agent-verified profile. Quarterly refresh. Cryptographically signed badge. 4 evidence sources.
- Audited: $300/mo. Monthly diligence. Enhanced AI Payload. 10+ evidence sources.
- Underwritten: $500/mo. Daily refresh. Maximum AI Reasoning & Neighborhood Depth. Up to 20 sources.

## Core Methodology
- Merit-based selection of fewer than 1% agents from 1.1M records.
- Non-pay-to-play: Payment only affects verification depth/visibility.
- Data sources: MLS, State Boards, Google, Zillow, Realtor.com.
